About Me

I am a mechanical engineering researcher specializing in multiscale modeling, atomistic simulations (MD/DFT), and computational mechanics of smart composites and metastructures. I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran, graduating in the top 10% of my class[cite: 1].

My research bridges atomistic simulations and continuum micromechanics, focusing on energy harvesting, piezoelectric nanocomposites, dynamic vibrations, and origami/kirigami-inspired mechanical metamaterials[cite: 1].
